# Warm Light

## Short description

Turn glass pipes, guide a warm glow home, and solve cozy puzzles before the charge runs out.

## Description

A quiet courtyard. Dark windows. One little spark waiting to find its way home.

Connect the pipes and send electricity from the transformer to the house. Collect stars along the way and finish before your charge runs out. Each of the 26 levels adds a new twist: relay crossovers, underground passages, sliding pipes, linked levers and sudden line breaks.

Take a hint when you need a hand, replay levels to collect more stars, and bring a little more light to the neighbourhood.

- 26 handcrafted pipe puzzles
- A cozy evening atmosphere and gentle music
- Short, interactive tutorials
- Touch controls for phones and tablets, plus mouse controls on computers
- English and Russian interfaces

## How to play

Tap a pipe to turn it. Build a continuous path from the transformer to the house, passing through stars when you can. Tap the transformer to send the charge.

Some pipes are fixed, some slide, and others move together through a lever. Watch the short tutorial when a new mechanic appears.

Your charge is limited. If it runs out, one fuse burns out and the pipes shuffle. Tap the bulb for a hint or the fuse button to get more attempts.

Change your language using the globe button in the main menu or Language in Settings. Your progress stays with you.
